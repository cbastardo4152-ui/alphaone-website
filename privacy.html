Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

AlphaOne Business Automation LLC respects your privacy. This policy describes how we handle information submitted to us through our website and our direct business communications. Separate client-specific privacy practices may apply to reception services we operate for other businesses.

Information we collect

When you contact us, we may collect your name, business name, email address, telephone number, inquiry details, appointment preferences, and any other information you voluntarily submit. Our website hosting provider may process basic technical information needed to deliver the site.

How we use information

We use contact information to respond to inquiries, arrange requested meetings, provide agreed services, communicate operational updates, maintain records, and comply with applicable obligations. We send transactional text messages only where an appropriate consent or other lawful basis exists.

Mobile information and SMS consent

We do not sell or share mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in consent information with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Information may be processed by service providers solely to deliver requested communications and operate our services, subject to appropriate protections. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with third parties for their own marketing.

Service providers and security

We may use hosting, communications, scheduling, and business software providers to process information on our behalf. We use reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to our operations, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and your choices

We retain information as reasonably needed for the purposes above and applicable legal requirements. You may request access, correction, or deletion where applicable by contacting us. To stop AlphaOne text messages, reply STOP.
